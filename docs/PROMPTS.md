## Batch 1 — style frames (waits on Javier's pick of a free frame first)

**Count: 6 pictures** (the picked direction, 2 takes of each of 3 moments). **Cost: $0 extra** (ChatGPT Plus).

Start the chat with the shared rules, then one prompt per picture.

> **Shared rules** (paste first): "We're making the art for an original 2D side-scrolling street brawler. Every
> picture is 16:9, 1920×1080, drawn **perfectly side-on with no perspective**, like a stage set seen from the front.
> The street is the bottom third. Night, after rain. The palette is deep indigo sky `#1b1035`, magenta neon `#ff3ea5`,
> cyan neon `#2de2e6`, wet asphalt `#23202f` with mirror reflections of the signs. Fighters are about a third of the
> screen tall. No text that spells real brand names, no logos, no blood, no guns. Original characters only; don't
> imitate any existing game, film or artist."

> **Direction A, "Neon ink"**: "Bold dark outlines, flat colour in two tones per shape, a thin rim of magenta or
> cyan light on every edge that faces a sign. Clean, readable, poster-like."

> **Direction B, "Soft glow"**: "No outlines. Shapes are painted with smooth gradients and soft glow around every light;
> the fighters are lit by the signs with soft rim light. Dreamy and atmospheric, still crisp at the edges."

> **Direction C, "Grit comic"**: "A broken, inky comic line, heavy black shadows, a fine halftone dot texture in the
> mid tones, a slightly limited palette. Punchy and rough, like a printed comic page."

Moments (one prompt each, after the direction): (1) "The hero, a young man in a dark jacket and sneakers, in a low
fighting stance on the left; a thug in a tank top facing him on the right; a noodle bar and a closed pawn shop behind
them." (2) "The same street, empty, a wide view: the block across the street with signs, fire escapes and shutters, the
skyline behind." (3) "The hero landing a high side kick on the thug, who reels back; puddles splash."

## Batch 2 — the fighters as parts sheets (after Batch 1 is picked)

**Count: 16 pictures** (4 fighters × 4 sheets: the parts, then bruised, swollen, broken). **Cost: $0 extra.**

> "Using the picked style, draw [the fighter] as a **cut-out parts sheet** for a 2D puppet, side-on facing right, on a
> flat `#00ff00` green background with space between every piece: head, neck, chest, belly, upper arm, forearm, a fist
> and an open hand, thigh, shin, foot, toes; one of each for the near side and the far side (the far side a little
> darker). Each piece has a rounded overlap at its joints. Two tones plus the magenta/cyan rim light. Same scale on
> every sheet: the whole body would stand 1,100 px tall."
>
> Damage sheets: "The same parts sheet, same layout, now **bruised** (purple and yellow patches, a split lip)" /
> "**swollen** (an eye swollen shut, puffy cheek, swollen knuckles)" / "**broken** (the forearm and shin bent at a wrong
> angle at the middle, a crack line; no blood anywhere)."

Fighters: the hero (a man), the hero (a woman), a thug (big, tank top, chain), a knife fighter (lean, hoodie).
